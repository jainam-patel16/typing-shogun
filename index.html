The way of the blade

Type the word on a shadow to cut it down. Your first letter locks onto the nearest match; finish the word to strike. Anything that reaches you costs a life.

Streak tiers

Wave clear
100 × wave number, doubled if you lost no life that wave.

Generals

Every fifth wave a general marches in carrying a whole phrase. Type it, spaces included. Cutting one down restores a life (up to 5).

Multiplayer

Up to 4 players on their own devices share the same waves. Hitting a 10 streak, or cutting an elite or a general, sends a cursed shadow to every rival. When the last samurai falls, the highest score wins.

Coins and avatars

Every word you cut earns 1 coin. Big words (8+ letters) earn 2, elites 3, and generals 10. Cut three words within 2 seconds for a triple slash worth 3 bonus coins. A flawless wave adds 5, and winning a duel adds 20. Spend coins in the avatar dojo to unlock new samurai.

Grades

S needs 97% accuracy and 45 words per minute. A is 93% and 35, B is 88% and 25, C is 80%. Everything else is D.
